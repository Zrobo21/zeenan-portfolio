A lot of small businesses in Bangladesh treat their Facebook page as the whole website. That's not necessarily wrong — Facebook is where the customers are. The problem is most of these pages are invisible outside Facebook itself, because nobody set them up to be found by Google.

## Your page name matters more than you think

If your page is named just "Rahim's Shop," Google has almost nothing to work with. A page named "Rahim's Shop — Custom Furniture in Mirpur, Dhaka" gives search engines an actual reason to show it for furniture-related searches in that area. Don't stuff it with keywords awkwardly, but do include what you sell and where, naturally.

## The "About" section is underused SEO real estate

Most business owners write one line here, if that. This section actually gets indexed and can appear in search snippets. Write two or three full sentences describing what you do, who you serve, and where — the same way you'd describe your business to a stranger who's never heard of it.

## Consistency across platforms builds trust signals

If your business is called "Zara's Boutique" on Facebook but "Zara Boutique BD" on Google Maps and "Zaras_Boutique" on Instagram, search engines struggle to confirm these are the same business. Pick one name, one phone number format, one address format, and use it everywhere. This consistency is one of the strongest local SEO signals that costs nothing to fix.

## Posts do get indexed — write them like it

Facebook posts can show up in Google search results, particularly for specific product or service mentions. A post that just says "New stock coming soon!" gives Google nothing to match a search query against. A post like "New winter jacket collection now available at our Gulshan branch, sizes S to XXL" gives both your followers and search engines something concrete.

## Reviews compound over time

Reviews aren't just social proof for visitors — they're a ranking signal, and they contain natural language full of the exact phrases customers use to describe your business. A steady trickle of genuine reviews, even five or ten a month, outperforms a burst of fifty reviews once and then silence.

## FAQ

**Does a Facebook page actually rank on Google, or does it stay inside Facebook?**
Facebook pages and even individual posts can and do appear in Google search results, especially for branded searches (someone searching your business name) and increasingly for local service queries too.

**I don't have a website. Is Facebook SEO enough on its own?**
It can carry you further than most business owners expect, but it has a ceiling. A basic website, even one page, gives you control over structure and keywords that a Facebook page's fixed layout doesn't allow.

**How often should I post for this to make a difference?**
Consistency matters more than frequency. Two to three well-written posts a week that actually describe products or services beats daily posts that are just generic engagement bait.

**Does the page category I choose matter for SEO?**
Yes. Facebook's category system feeds into how the page gets classified and surfaced, both on Facebook and in search results. Choose the most specific category available rather than a broad one.
